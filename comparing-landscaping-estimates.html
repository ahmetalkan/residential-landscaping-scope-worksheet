Comparing Residential Landscaping Estimates Apples to Apples

Two landscapers can bid the same backyard sketch with wildly different totals simply because one specified 1-gallon starter shrubs over existing soil while the other priced 5-gallon specimens, compost tilling, metal edging, and debris haul-away.

Line items to normalize across bids

Bid itemDetail to specify in writing
Plant listBotanical name, exact quantity, and container size (1-gal, 3-gal, 5-gal, or tree caliper inches)
Soil & mulchCubic yards of topsoil/compost tilled in and mulch type/depth
Demolition & disposalWhether old sod, concrete rubble, and green-waste dump fees are included
Irrigation adjustmentsRelocating or capping existing sprinkler heads to fit new bed shapes
Plant care responsibilityWritten watering instructions and clear replacement terms if a tree fails to establish
